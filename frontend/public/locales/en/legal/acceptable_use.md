# Acceptable Use Policy

This policy outlines the acceptable use of the **CoopData** platform. By accessing or using the service, you agree to:

- **Comply with all applicable laws** and regulations.
- **Respect the rights** of other users, including privacy and intellectual property.
- **Avoid any activity** that could disrupt, degrade, or compromise the platform, such as:
  - Unauthorized access or hacking attempts.
  - Distribution of malware, viruses, or harmful code.
  - Spamming, harassment, or abusive language.
- **Use the service** only for legitimate cooperative data management purposes and not for illegal commercial activities.

Violations may result in suspension or termination of your account, and may be reported to authorities.
