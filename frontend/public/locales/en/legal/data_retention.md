# Data Retention & Erasure Policy

**Effective Date:** September 10, 2026  
**Version:** 1.0  

This Data Retention Policy defines how long CoopData retains personal, financial, and organizational data, and outlines procedures for data deletion and archiving.

---

## 1. Retention Principles
CoopData retains data only for the period necessary to fulfill the purposes for which it was collected, including satisfying legal, accounting, or regulatory reporting obligations.

## 2. Retention Schedules

| Data Category | Retention Period | Statutory Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| **Financial Statements & Filings** | 10 Years | National Cooperative Audit & Tax Regulations |
| **Cooperative Member Records** | Duration of active membership + 5 Years | Statutory Record Keeping & Member Disputes |
| **Audit Logs & Consent Records** | 7 Years | Security Compliance & Regulatory Auditability |
| **User Account Credentials** | Active Account + 30 Days post-termination | Account Recovery & Security Verification |
| **Offline Drafts & Temporary Files** | 30 Days | Cache Cleanup & System Optimization |

## 3. Data Deletion & Anonymization
Upon receipt of an approved account deletion request or expiration of mandatory retention periods:
- Personal identifiers (name, email, phone) are permanently anonymized or deleted.
- Aggregated financial metrics remain in historical benchmark datasets without personal identification.

## 4. Submitting Erasure Requests
Users may initiate data export or erasure requests under **Profile > Privacy & Security Settings**.
