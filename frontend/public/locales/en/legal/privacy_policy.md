# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** September 10, 2026  
**Version:** 1.0  

CoopData ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting the privacy, security, and confidentiality of personal and organizational data processed through the CoopData platform. This Privacy Policy details how we collect, use, store, and safeguard your information in compliance with international and regional data protection standards (including GDPR, NDPR, and CCPA).

---

## 1. Information We Collect
We collect the following categories of information:
- **Account & Identity Data:** Full name, email address, phone number, designated user role, organizational affiliation (Cooperative, Apex, Federation, or Ministry), and authentication credentials.
- **Member & Co-op Financial Data:** Member rosters, savings and loan balances, crop/farm inputs, financial statements, and survey/questionnaire responses.
- **Audit & Technical Data:** IP addresses, browser user-agent strings, device identifiers, login timestamps, and system audit logs.

## 2. How We Use Information
Your data is processed strictly for legitimate operational purposes:
1. Validating user identity and enforcing role-based access boundaries.
2. Generating aggregated sector benchmarks, compliance reports, and financial health indicators.
3. Facilitating statutory filings between cooperatives, apex organizations, federations, and ministries.
4. Maintaining audit trails for data integrity, security monitoring, and legal compliance.

## 3. Data Sharing & Disclosure
We do not sell, rent, or trade personal or organizational data. Data is shared exclusively:
- **Within the Organizational Hierarchy:** Based on role boundaries (e.g., Apexes view member co-ops; Ministries view consolidated national reports).
- **With Service Providers:** Trusted cloud hosting and authentication providers operating under strict confidentiality contracts.
- **Legal Compliance:** When required by law, subpoena, or statutory regulation.

## 4. Your Rights (Data Subject Rights)
Under applicable data privacy laws, you have the following rights:
- **Right of Access:** View the personal data and consent records associated with your account.
- **Right to Data Portability (Export):** Submit a request to export your personal data in a structured, machine-readable format.
- **Right to Rectification:** Request corrections to inaccurate personal or organizational details.
- **Right to Erasure (Deletion):** Request account deletion or data anonymization (subject to statutory retention obligations for financial records).

You may exercise these rights via the **Privacy & Security Settings** tab in your user profile or by submitting a privacy request.

## 5. Security & Data Retention
- We implement AES-256 encryption at rest, TLS 1.3 encryption in transit, and multi-factor authentication (MFA).
- Personal data is retained only as long as necessary for account operation and statutory record-keeping obligations.

## 6. Updates to This Policy
Material updates will be notified in-app. Continued use of the platform after policy updates signifies your acknowledgment and acceptance.
