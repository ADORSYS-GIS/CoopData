# Data Processing Governance & Roles

**Proposed effective date:** 1 October 2026

## 1. Purpose

This internal/customer-facing governance document clarifies responsibilities between CoopData, Customers, authorized Users and service providers. It should be used together with the Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.

## 2. Customer responsibilities

- Determine which cooperative/organizational information may be collected and for what authorized purposes.
- Ensure lawful collection and submission of personal information.
- Provide appropriate notices to individuals where required.
- Define authorized users and access levels.
- Review and correct inaccurate information.
- Respond to data-subject requests where the Customer is the controller.
- Notify CoopData promptly of suspected unauthorized access or unlawful disclosure.

## 3. CoopData responsibilities

- Operate and secure the Platform using reasonable technical and organizational measures.
- Process Customer Data according to documented instructions where acting as processor.
- Maintain consent and acceptance records where the Platform is responsible for doing so.
- Support correction, export, deletion and incident-response workflows.
- Use service providers under appropriate contractual and security controls.
- Maintain relevant processing, vendor and incident records.

## 4. User responsibilities

- Use only authorized accounts and data.
- Keep credentials confidential.
- Verify submissions before finalization.
- Do not upload information without authority.
- Follow the Acceptable Use Policy.
- Report errors, suspected breaches and unauthorized access promptly.

## 5. Data controller/processor allocation

The parties should document the controller/processor allocation for each implementation. Where CoopData acts as processor, the Customer’s documented instructions should specify subject matter, duration, nature, purpose, data categories, data-subject categories, security requirements, subprocessing and deletion/return requirements.

## 6. Subprocessors

CoopData may appoint subprocessors to provide infrastructure or specialized services. Where legally required, Customers will receive appropriate information about subprocessors and relevant changes, and contractual safeguards will be maintained.

## 7. International processing

The implementation team must maintain a current record of countries/regions in which data is hosted, accessed or supported, and confirm that cross-border transfers satisfy applicable legal requirements.

## 8. Records and audit

CoopData should maintain records of material processing activities, consents, policy versions, security incidents, subprocessors and relevant Customer instructions to support accountability.
