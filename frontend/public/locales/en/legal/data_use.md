# Data Use, Aggregation & Consent Notice

**Proposed effective date:** 1 October 2026

## 1. Purpose

This notice governs the optional permission for CoopData and authorized project partners to use eligible Platform information to produce aggregated, statistical, benchmarking, research, monitoring, learning and development outputs.

## 2. What may be used

- Cooperative-level indicators such as membership totals, savings, loans, governance indicators, inclusion indicators, digitalization indicators and other approved metrics.
- Technical and usage information necessary to assess Platform performance, provided the use complies with the Privacy Policy.
- Information specifically marked by the Customer as eligible for consolidated reporting.

## 3. Aggregation and de-identification

Where data is used for consolidated reporting, CoopData will seek to combine information across multiple organizations and apply reasonable aggregation/de-identification measures so that individual persons are not identified and individual cooperatives are not identifiable where the reporting purpose does not require identification.

Small-cell or unusually specific results should be suppressed, combined or otherwise treated where necessary to reduce re-identification risk.

## 4. Permitted outputs

- Sector-level dashboards and reports.
- Aggregated benchmarks and trend analyses.
- Donor/development-programme monitoring and learning reports.
- Research, training and knowledge products.
- Internal product and methodology improvement using aggregated or de-identified information.
- Public communication only where the relevant data is sufficiently aggregated/de-identified or separate authorization has been obtained.

## 5. Identifiable publication

CoopData will not publish a cooperative’s identifiable financial, operational or organizational information as a consolidated/public output merely because the cooperative uses the Platform. Identifiable publication requires a separate lawful basis and, where appropriate, specific authorization from the relevant data controller/organization.

## 6. No sale of raw Customer Data

CoopData will not sell raw Customer Data as a commercial data product. Any commercial or externally licensed analytical product involving identifiable Customer Data requires separate written authorization and appropriate contractual terms.

## 7. Withdrawal

Where this permission is based on consent, the Customer/User may withdraw consent for future optional uses through the designated mechanism or by contacting CoopData. Withdrawal does not invalidate processing already lawfully completed and does not affect processing that has another lawful basis.

## 8. Recommended Platform consent record

- Consent category: Aggregated/De-identified Sector Reporting.
- Decision: Accepted / Declined / Withdrawn.
- Policy version accepted.
- User/account identifier.
- Organization identifier.
- Date and time.
- Relevant source/interface.
- Evidence of the consent action.

## 9. Suggested user-facing checkbox

> ☐ I authorize CoopData, where legally permitted, to use eligible data from my organization for aggregated, statistical, benchmarking, research, monitoring and development reporting. Reports will be designed to avoid identifying individuals and, unless separately authorized, will not publish identifiable information about my organization.
