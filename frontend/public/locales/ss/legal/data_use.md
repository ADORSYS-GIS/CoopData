# Satiso Sekusetjentiswa Nekuhlanganiswa Kwemininingwane Nemvume

**Lusuku loluhlongotwako lwekucala kusebenta:** 1 iMphala 2026

## 1. Inhloso

Lesatiso silawula imvume lengasiso sibopho levumela i-CoopData kanye nalabasebentisana nayo kumaphrojekthi labagunyatiwe kutsi basebentise imininingwane yeNkundla lefanelekile kute bakhicite imiphumela lehlanganisiwe, yetibalo, yekucatsanisa, yelucwaningo, yekulandzelela, yekufundza neyentfutfuko.

## 2. Lokungasetjentiswa

- Tinkhomba temazinga etinhlangano tekubambisana njengenani lonkhe lemalunga, kulondvoloza, timalimboleko, tinkhomba tekuphatsa, tinkhomba tekufakwa kwabo bonkhe, tinkhomba tekusebentisa buchwepheshe kanye naletinye tilinganiso letivunyiwe.
- Imininingwane yebuchwepheshe neyekusebentisa ledzingekako kute kuhlolwe kusebenta kweNkundla, inkhulu nje kutsi kusetjentiswa kwayo kulandzela Inchubomgomo Yebumfihlo.
- Imininingwane Likhasimende leyibeke ngalokucacile njengalefanelekela kubikwa lokuhlanganisiwe.

## 3. Kuhlanganisa nekususa bunjalo bemuntu

Lapho imininingwane isetjentiselwa kubika lokuhlanganisiwe, i-CoopData itawuzama kuhlanganisa imininingwane yetinhlangano letinyenti futsi isebentise tinyatselo letifanele tekuhlanganisa/tekususa bunjalo kute bantfu bangatiwa futsi tinhlangano tekubambisana ngayinye tingatiwa lapho inhloso yekubika ingakudzingi kutiwa.

Imiphumela lemayelana nemacembu lamancane kakhulu noma lecondzile ngendlela lengakajwayeleki kufanele icindzetelwe, ihlanganiswe noma iphatfwe ngalenye indlela lapho kudzingeka khona kute kunciphe ingoti yekuphindze kutiwe.

## 4. Imiphumela levunyelwe

- Emadeshibhodi nemibiko yemkhakha wonkhe.
- Kucatsanisa lokuhlanganisiwe nekuhlatiya kwemikhuba.
- Imibiko yekulandzelela neyekufundza yebaniketi betimali/tinhlelo tentfutfuko.
- Imikhicito yelucwaningo, yekucecesha neyelwati.
- Kuthutfukiswa kwangekhatsi kwemikhicito netindlela tekusebenta kusetjentiswa imininingwane lehlanganisiwe noma lesusiwe bunjalo bemuntu.
- Kuchumana nemphakatsi kuphela lapho imininingwane lefanele ihlanganiswe/isuswe bunjalo ngalokwanele noma lapho kutfolwe imvume lehlukile.

## 5. Kushicilela lokuveta bunjalo

I-CoopData ngeke ishicilele imininingwane yetimali, yekusebenta noma yenhlangano leveta bunjalo benhlangano yekubambisana njengemphumela lohlanganisiwe/wemphakatsi ngenca nje yekutsi leyo nhlangano yekubambisana isebentisa Inkundla. Kushicilela lokuveta bunjalo kudzinga sisekelo semtsetfo lesehlukile futsi, lapho kufanele khona, imvume lecondzile levela kumlawuli wemininingwane/enhlanganweni lefanele.

## 6. Kute kutsengiswa kweMininingwane yeLikhasimende lengakacubungulwa

I-CoopData ngeke itsengise Imininingwane yeLikhasimende lengakacubungulwa njengemkhicito wemininingwane wekuhweba. Nanobe ngumuphi umkhicito wekuhlatiya wekuhweba noma lonikwe lilayisensi ngaphandle lofaka ekhatsi Imininingwane yeLikhasimende leveta bunjalo udzinga imvume lebhaliwe lehlukile kanye nemibandzela yesivumelwano lefanele.

## 7. Kuhoca imvume

Lapho lemvume isekelwe esivumelwaneni, Likhasimende/Umsebentisi angahoca imvume yakhe ngekusetjentiswa kwesikhatsi lesitako lokungasiso sibopho ngendlela lebekiwe noma ngekutsintsana ne-CoopData. Kuhoca akukwenti kutsi kucubungula lokwesekwentiwe ngalokusemtsetfweni kube kute, futsi akukutsikameti kucubungula lokunalesinye sisekelo semtsetfo.

## 8. Lirekhodi lemvume leliphakanyiswako eNkundleni

- Luhlobo lwemvume: Kubika Ngemkhakha Lokuhlanganisiwe/Lokususiwe Bunjalo Bemuntu.
- Sincumo: Kwemukeliwe / Kwaliwe / Kuhociwe.
- Inguculo yenchubomgomo leyemukeliwe.
- Siphawu semsebentisi/se-akhawunti.
- Siphawu senhlangano.
- Lusuku nesikhatsi.
- Umtfombo/i-interface lefanele.
- Bufakazi besento semvume.

## 9. Libhokisi lekumaka leliphakanyiswako kulabasebentisi

> ☐ Ngiyayigunyata i-CoopData, lapho kuvunyelwe ngumtsetfo, kutsi isebentise imininingwane lefanelekile yenhlangano yami ekubikeni lokuhlanganisiwe, kwetibalo, kwekucatsanisa, kwelucwaningo, kwekulandzelela nekwentfutfuko. Imibiko itawakhiwa ngendlela legwema kuveta bantfu futsi, ngaphandle kwekutsi kunemvume lehlukile, ngeke ishicilele imininingwane leveta bunjalo benhlangano yami.
