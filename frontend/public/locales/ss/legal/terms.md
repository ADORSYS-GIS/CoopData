# Imigomo Yekusebentisa

**Lusuku loluhlongotwako lwekucala kusebenta:** 1 iMphala 2026

## 1. Tinhlangotsi nekwemukela

Le Migomo Yekusebentisa (“Imigomo”) ilawula kungena nekusebentisa inkundla ye-CoopData (“Inkundla”) lesetjentiswa yi-DGRV kanye neLitiko leTekuhweba, Timboni Nekuhwebelana (Ministry of Commerce, Trade and Industries), Umnyango Wekutfutfukisa Tinhlangano Tekubambisana. Ku- __________ (“CoopData”, “tsine” noma “yetfu”).

Ngekuvula i-akhawunti, ngekungena eNkundleni, noma ngekuchofoza inkinobho yekwemukela nayivelile, umsebentisi (“Umsebentisi”) uyayemukela le Migomo.

Uma inhlangano yenta sivumelwano ne-CoopData, leyo nhlangano “Likhasimende” futsi le Migomo isebenta kanye nesivumelwano lesifanele. Uma kunekungcubutana, sivumelwano lesisayiniwe siyaphumelela ngalokulingana nalokungcubutana.

## 2. Kufaneleka nekusebentisa lokuvunyelwe

- Basebentisi kufanele banikete imininingwane yekubhalisa leyiliciniso futsi kufanele bagunyatwe kutsi basebentele inhlangano labayimelele.
- Basebentisi kufanele bagcine imininingwane yabo yekungena iyimfihlo futsi akukafanele babelane ngema-akhawunti ngaphandle kwekutsi Inkundla ivumela ngalokucacile kungena lokunikelwe kulomunye.
- Basebentisi banemtfwalo ngaloko lokwentiwa nge-akhawunti yabo, ngaphandle kwalapho kubangelwe kwephula kwe-CoopData uqobo noma kungena lokungakagunyatwa lokuyicala le-CoopData.
- Basebentisi kufanele balandzele imitsetfo, imitsetfwana, timfuneko temkhakha wetinhlangano tekubambisana, tibopho tesivumelwano kanye netinchubomgomo tangekhatsi letisebentako.

## 3. Inhloso yeNkundla

I-CoopData yinkundla yekucoca, kulawula, kuhlatiya nekubika imininingwane leyentelwe kusekela kulawulwa kwemininingwane yemkhakha wetinhlangano tekubambisana, kulandzelela, kuhlela, lucwaningo, kucatsanisa kanye netinsita tentfutfuko letihambisana nako. I-CoopData ayihloli ngekutimela yonkhe imininingwane lefakwa ngulabasebentisi futsi ayiniketi teluleko tekubalwa kwetimali, tekuhlolwa kwetimali (audit), tetemtsetfo, tentsela, tekutjala imali noma tekulawula ngekutsi nje yetfula imininingwane noma imibiko.

## 4. Imininingwane yeLikhasimende nebunikati

Likhasimende kanye/noma Umsebentisi ugcina emalungelo akhe kumininingwane layifakako (“Imininingwane yeLikhasimende”), ngaphansi kwetimvume letinikwe i-CoopData kule Migomo nakusivumelwano lesisebentako. Umsebentisi uyacinisekisa kutsi unemandla kanye netimvume tonkhe letidzingekako kutsi afake Imininingwane yeLikhasimende futsi avumele i-CoopData kutsi iyicubungule ngetinhloso letishiwo.

I-CoopData ingacubungula Imininingwane yeLikhasimende kute isebentise, ivikele, inakekele, ichubekisele embili futsi isekele Inkundla; yente imibiko leceliwe; ilungise tinkinga futsi ihlole kusebenta; ilandzele umtsetfo; futsi yakhe tibalo letihlanganisiwe noma letisusiwe bunjalo bemuntu njengobe kuchazwe Esimvumeni Sekusetjentiswa Nekuhlanganiswa Kwemininingwane.

## 5. Kuba yiliciniso nemtfwalo wetintfo letifakwako

- Basebentisi banemtfwalo wekutsi imininingwane labayifakako isemtsetfweni, iliciniso, iphelele futsi ifike ngesikhatsi.
- Basebentisi akukafanele bafake imininingwane labangakavunyelwa kuyiveta.
- Uma imininingwane ifakwa egameni lenhlangano yekubambisana noma lenye inhlangano, Umsebentisi uyacinisekisa kutsi leyo nhlangano ikugunyatile kufakwa kwayo kanye netinhloso letishiwo.
- Basebentisi kufanele babuyekete imibiko ngaphambi kwekutsi bayetsembe ekutseni tincumo tekusebenta, tetimali, tekulawula noma tekuphatsa.

## 6. Bunikati bengcondvo

Inkundla, i-software, ema-interface, imibhalo, luphawu, emathempulethi, tindlela tekusebenta, imibiko lekhicitwa luhlelo njengekusebenta kweluhlelo, kanye nethekhnoloji lengaphansi kwako kungeye-DGRV - CoopData noma kunikwe yona ngelilayisensi, futsi kuvikelwe yimitsetfo yebunikati bengcondvo lesebentako. Le Migomo ayidlulisi bunikati beNkundla kulabasebentisi.

Basebentisi bawutfola lilungelo lelincane, lelingasilo lekukhetsa, lelingadluliselwa kulomunye lekusebentisa Inkundla ngetinhloso letigunyatiwe teLikhasimende ngesikhatsi sekubhalisela, sesiphrojekthi noma sekungena lesisebentako.

## 7. Kusebentisa lokwemukelekako

Basebentisi kufanele balandzele Inchubomgomo Yekusebentisa Lokwemukelekako ye-CoopData. Kutiphatsa lokwenqatjelwe kufaka ekhatsi kungena lokungakagunyatwa, kwabelana ngemininingwane yekungena, kuhlola kuvikeleka ngaphandle kwemvume, kudvonsa imininingwane ngemishini (scraping), emaphrogramu lalimatako (malware), kuzama kwedlula tinsita tekulawula kungena, kucubungula lokungekho emtsetfweni, kufaka imininingwane yenkhohliso, kuhlakata luhlelo kute kutfolakale indlela lolwakhiwe ngayo ngaphandle kwalapho kuvunyelwe ngumtsetfo, kanye nekusebentisa lokungaphazamisa noma kulimate Inkundla noma lomunye umsebentisi.

## 8. Kutfolakala nekushintja

I-CoopData itawenta imizamo lefanele kute Inkundla itfolakale kodvwa ayicinisekisi kusebenta lokungaphazamiseki noma lokungenamaphutsa. Kunakekelwa, tinyatselo tekuvikeleka, kubuyeketwa kweluhlelo, kwetsembela etinhlanganweni tangaphandle, kwehluleka kwekuchumana kanye netehlakalo letingaphandle kwekulawula lokufanele kungatsikameta kutfolakala.

I-CoopData ingashintja kusebenta kweNkundla nakudzingeka ngalokufanele kute kuvikeleke, kulandzelwe umtsetfo, kunakekelwe noma kuthutfukiswe. Lushintjo lolubalulekile kule Migomo lutawutjelwa basebentisi ngeNkundla noma ngaletinye tindlela letifanele.

## 9. Kugcinwa kwemfihlo

Lohlangotsi ngamunye kufanele luvikele imininingwane leyimfihlo lelutfole kulolunye luhlangotsi futsi luyisebentise kuphela ngetinhloso letigunyatiwe. Lesibopho asisebenti kumininingwane lesesidlangalaleni ngaphandle kwekwephula, leyentiwe ngekutimela, letfolwe ngalokusemtsetfweni kulomunye umtfombo, noma lekufanele iventwe ngekwemtsetfo. Uma umtsetfo uvuma, luhlangotsi lolubophekile kuveta imininingwane leyimfihlo lutawuniketa saziso kucala.

## 10. Tinsita tetinkampani tangaphandle

Inkundla ingetsembela kubaniketi betinsita tekugcina imininingwane, te-cloud, tekuchumana, tekuhlatiya, tekuvikeleka, tekucinisekisa bunjalo bemsebentisi noma letinye tinsita. Uma labaniketi betinsita bacubungula imininingwane yemuntu egameni le-CoopData, i-CoopData itawusebentisa tinyatselo tesivumelwano naletebuchwepheshe letifanele letihambisana netimfuneko tekuvikelwa kwemininingwane letisebentako.

## 11. Kuphika imitfwalo

Ngalokulingana nalokuvunyelwe ngumtsetfo, Inkundla iniketwa “ngendlela letfolakala ngayo”. I-CoopData ayicinisekisi kutsi yonkhe imininingwane lefakiwe iliciniso, iphelele noma ifanelekela sincumo lesitsite, noma kutsi Inkundla itawufeza yonkhe imfuneko yekusebenta letsite.

Kute lokukule Migomo lokukhipha imitfwalo lengeke yakhishwa ngekwemtsetfo, lokufaka ekhatsi imitfwalo levela enkhohlisweni noma kuletinye tibopho temtsetfo letisitjengiso.

## 12. Kukhawulelwa kwemitfwalo

Ngalokukhulu lokuvunyelwe ngumtsetfo losebentako, i-CoopData ngeke ibe nemtfwalo ngekulahlekelwa lokungacondzile, lokwenteka ngengoti, lokukhetsekile noma lokulandzelako lokuvela ekusebentiseni Inkundla, lokufaka ekhatsi kulahlekelwa lokubangelwe Imininingwane yeLikhasimende lengasilo liciniso lefakwe ngulabasebentisi, ngaphandle kwalapho lokukhawulela lokunjalo kwenqatjelwe ngumtsetfo. Nobe ngumuphi umkhawulo wemtfwalo wesivumelwano kufanele ufakwe lapha ngemuva kwekubuyeketwa ngetemtsetfo futsi uhambisane nesivumelwano seLikhasimende.

## 13. Kumiswa nekuphela

I-CoopData ingamisa kungena nakudzingeka ngalokufanele kute kuvikeleke, kulandzelwe umtsetfo, kuvinjelwe kusetjentiswa lokubi, kubhekanwe nekungakhokhi lapho kusebenta khona, noma kuphendvulwe kwephula lokubalulekile.

Basebentisi bangayekela kusebentisa Inkundla futsi bacele kutsi i-akhawunti/imininingwane yabo iphatfwe ngekuvumelana neNchubomgomo Yebumfihlo, Luhlelo Lwekugcinwa Nekucishwa kanye nesivumelwano seLikhasimende lesisebentako.

Kuphela akusisusi tibopho letitsi ngebunjalo bato tichubeke ngemuva kwekuphela, lokufaka ekhatsi kugcinwa kwemfihlo, bunikati bengcondvo, tibopho tekukhokha letesekwenteke kanye nekugcinwa lokudzingwa ngumtsetfo.
