# Kuphatsa Nemisebenti Ekucubungulweni Kwemininingwane

**Lusuku loluhlongotwako lwekucala kusebenta:** 1 iMphala 2026

## 1. Inhloso

Lombhalo wekuphatsa, wangekhatsi futsi lobhekiswe kumakhasimende, ucacisa imitfwalo ye-CoopData, Emakhasimende, Basebentisi labagunyatiwe kanye nebaniketi betinsita. Kufanele usetjentiswe kanye neMigomo Yekusebentisa kanye neNchubomgomo Yebumfihlo.

## 2. Imitfwalo yeLikhasimende

- Kuncuma kutsi ngumiphi imininingwane yenhlangano yekubambisana/yenhlangano lengacocwa futsi ngetiphi tinhloso letigunyatiwe.
- Kucinisekisa kutsi imininingwane yemuntu icocwa futsi ifakwa ngalokusemtsetfweni.
- Kuniketa bantfu tatiso letifanele lapho kudzingeka khona.
- Kuchaza labasebentisi labagunyatiwe kanye nemazinga ekungena.
- Kubuyeketa nekulungisa imininingwane lengasilo liciniso.
- Kuphendvula ticelo talabo labanemininingwane lapho Likhasimende lingumlawuli.
- Kutjela i-CoopData masinyane nakusolwa kungena lokungakagunyatwa noma kuvetwa lokungekho emtsetfweni.

## 3. Imitfwalo ye-CoopData

- Kusebentisa nekuvikela Inkundla kusetjentiswa tinyatselo letifanele tebuchwepheshe netenhlangano.
- Kucubungula Imininingwane yeLikhasimende ngekuya ngeticondziso letibhaliwe lapho isebenta njengemcubunguli.
- Kugcina emarekhodi emvume nekwemukela lapho Inkundla inemtfwalo wekwenta njalo.
- Kusekela tindlela tekulungisa, tekukhipha, tekucisha kanye netekuphendvula etehlakalweni.
- Kusebentisa baniketi betinsita ngaphansi kwetivikelo tesivumelwano netekuvikeleka letifanele.
- Kugcina emarekhodi lafanele ekucubungula, ebaniketi kanye netehlakalo.

## 4. Imitfwalo yeMsebentisi

- Sebentisa kuphela ema-akhawunti nemininingwane legunyatiwe.
- Gcina imininingwane yakho yekungena iyimfihlo.
- Hlola tintfo totifakako ngaphambi kwekutiphotfula.
- Ungalayishi imininingwane ngaphandle kwemvume.
- Landzela Inchubomgomo Yekusebentisa Lokwemukelekako.
- Bika masinyane emaphutsa, kwephulwa lokusolwako kanye nekungena lokungakagunyatwa.

## 5. Kwabelwa kwemisebenti yemlawuli nemcubunguli

Tinhlangotsi kufanele tibhale kwabelwa kwemisebenti yemlawuli nemcubunguli kukusetjentiswa ngakunye. Lapho i-CoopData isebenta njengemcubunguli, ticondziso letibhaliwe teLikhasimende kufanele ticacise ngendzaba, sikhatsi, bunjalo, inhloso, tinhlobo temininingwane, tinhlobo talabo labanemininingwane, timfuneko tekuvikeleka, kucubungulwa ngulabanye kanye netimfuneko tekucisha/tekubuyisela.

## 6. Bacubunguli labangetulu

I-CoopData ingaceshe bacubunguli labangetulu kute baniketi ingcalasizinda noma tinsita letikhetsekile. Lapho kudzingwa ngumtsetfo, Emakhasimende atawutfola imininingwane lefanele mayelana nebacubunguli labangetulu kanye nelushintjo lolufanele, futsi tivikelo tesivumelwano titawugcinwa.

## 7. Kucubungula emaveni angaphandle

Licembu lekusebentisa kufanele ligcine lirekhodi lelisesikhatsini lemave/tifundza lapho imininingwane igcinwa khona, lapho ingenwa khona noma isekelwa khona, futsi licinisekise kutsi kudluliselwa ngaphesheya kwemingcele kufeza timfuneko temtsetfo letisebentako.

## 8. Emarekhodi nekuhlolwa

I-CoopData kufanele igcine emarekhodi emisebenti lebalulekile yekucubungula, etimvume, etinguculo tetinchubomgomo, etehlakalo tekuvikeleka, ebacubunguli labangetulu kanye neticondziso teMakhasimende letifanele kute kusekelwe kuba nemtfwalo.
