# Federation guide

For federation officers. You oversee a set of apex organisations and follow the returns of the cooperatives below them. You do not approve submissions: the apex is the final approval level.

## What you can see

- **Dashboard:** the status of the returns across your apexes (waiting for the apex, approved, sent back).
- **Apexes:** the apex organisations under your federation.
- **Submissions:** every submission below your apexes except drafts, chosen by apex and then by cooperative. This is a read-only follow-up page.
- **Reports, Analytics, Basic Analytics, Benchmarking, Basic Benchmarking:** figures for your federation.
- **Users:** the accounts of each apex.
- **Admin legal:** the legal documents shown to users.
- **Data privacy & consents** and **Profile**.

## Manage apexes

Open **Apexes**.

- The cards show the number of apexes, the cooperatives across them, the apexes that have at least one cooperative, and the average number of cooperatives per apex.
- **Register apex** creates a new apex organisation. Give it a name and a description.
- Search the directory by name or description, edit an apex, or open its members.
- Deleting an apex removes what sits under it. The system asks for extra confirmation first (typing the name and re-entering your password).

## Manage apex members

Open **Users**, choose an apex, and use **Invite member** to invite an Apex Officer. The person gets an email to verify the address and set a password. From the same page you can resend the verification, update or remove a member.

## Follow submissions

1. Open **Submissions** and choose an apex, then a cooperative.
2. Open a submission to read its data, its status and its review history.

You see every submission that has left the draft stage, including those the apex has already approved. You cannot approve, return or reject a submission. If a return needs a correction, contact the apex that holds it.

## Download a report in your language

Reports are PDFs, available in English, French, Portuguese and siSwati. This applies to both report editions (financial statements and questionnaire).

1. Open **Reports** and choose what you want to export.
2. Pick the language. It starts on the language of the app.
3. Press **Download**. If the report is ready, it downloads at once.

If the language has not been prepared yet, the button shows "Preparing your report..." and the file downloads by itself when it is ready. This can take a short while, because the text is translated on request. English is always prepared first. If you close the page while it works, a notification with a Download button appears when the report is ready.

The small **Update report** link rebuilds the English report from the latest data. The other languages are then cleared and prepared again the next time someone asks for them. If preparation fails, a message asks you to try again; the button stays available.
