# Ministry guide

For Ministry officials. You see the whole country, follow every submission, read the national analytics and reports, and configure the platform. You do not approve submissions: the apex is the final approval level.

## What you can see

| Group | Pages |
| --- | --- |
| Oversight | Dashboard, Federations, Invitations, Members, Submissions |
| Intelligence | Reports, Analytics, Basic Analytics, Benchmarking, Basic Benchmarking, Custom KPIs |
| System | Settings, Configure Levels, Questionnaire Forms, Audit Log, Data privacy & consents, Profile |

## Dashboard

The dashboard shows national figures. Only submissions approved by an apex feed these figures. The filters let you narrow by federation, apex, region, sector or cooperative. Read [How the numbers are built](analytics-explained.md) before you compare two screens.

## Federations and invitations

- **Federations:** register a federation (name, domain and contact email), edit it, enable or disable it, or delete it. The domain is required for the sign-in system, so use one the organisation owns. Deleting a federation removes everything below it. The system asks you to type the name and to confirm your password first.
- **Invitations:** choose a federation and invite a **Federation Officer**. The list shows whether each invitee has verified the email address or is still waiting. You can resend or cancel an invitation.
- **Members:** the accounts of each federation, with their status (Active or Pending).

## Following submissions

Open **Submissions**. You can browse by federation, apex and cooperative, and open any submission that has left the draft stage.

- You see its data, its status and its full review history: who approved or returned it, with the comment and the time.
- A submission is approved when its apex approves it. Nothing is waiting for the Ministry.
- You cannot approve, return or reject a submission from this page. If something looks wrong, contact the apex that approved it.

## Intelligence

- **Reports:** generate and download the reports for the whole ecosystem. Reports are PDFs available in English, French, Portuguese and siSwati (see [Download a report in your language](#download-a-report-in-your-language)). When you export, you first choose the **report edition**: financial statements (SACCOs) or questionnaire (multipurpose). Only an edition that has approved data for the selected year can be chosen.
- **Analytics and Benchmarking:** SACCOs. **Basic Analytics and Basic Benchmarking:** multipurpose cooperatives.
- **Custom KPIs:** build your own indicator from existing ones with a formula, for example an adjusted ratio. Give it a name and a description, pick variables, and save. It is then available alongside the standard KPIs.

## Download a report in your language

Reports are PDFs, available in English, French, Portuguese and siSwati. This applies to both report editions (financial statements and questionnaire).

1. Open **Reports** and choose what you want to export.
2. Pick the language. It starts on the language of the app.
3. Press **Download**. If the report is ready, it downloads at once.

If the language has not been prepared yet, the button shows "Preparing your report..." and the file downloads by itself when it is ready. This can take a short while, because the text is translated on request. English is always prepared first. If you close the page while it works, a notification with a Download button appears when the report is ready.

The small **Update report** link rebuilds the English report from the latest data. The other languages are then cleared and prepared again the next time someone asks for them. If preparation fails, a message asks you to try again; the button stays available.

## System

- **Settings:** appearance and shortcuts to the configuration pages, and the **non-financial indicator catalogue** (periodic indicators such as board composition, training and audits that cooperatives must report).
- **Configure Levels:** rename the labels shown for each organisation level across the whole application.
- **Questionnaire Forms:** edit the Financial and Non-Financial questionnaires that multipurpose cooperatives fill in. You can add and reorder sections, add questions, mark them required and choose the answer type (for example dropdown or paragraph).
- **Audit Log:** every create, update, invite and delete on the platform, with the time, the actor and the IP address. Filter by action, resource type or actor.

## Legal documents

The legal documents (Terms of Service, Privacy Policy, Data Retention and others) are versioned, and every acceptance is stored with the version and the time. The wording shown to users lives in the legal centre, and the federation role has an **Admin legal** page to edit it.
