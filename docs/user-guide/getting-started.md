# Getting started

This page applies to every role.

## Signing in

1. Open the CoopData address your organisation gave you and choose **Sign in**.
2. You are sent to the secure sign-in page. Enter your email and password.
3. If two-step verification is on for your account, enter the 6-digit code from your authenticator app.

Your account is created by an invitation. The invitation email asks you to verify your email address and set your password. The password checklist on the sign-in pages turns green as you meet each rule. If the email does not arrive, ask the person who invited you to resend it.

After signing in you land on your dashboard. What you see depends on your role.

## First sign-in: legal agreements

The first time you sign in you are asked to accept the **Terms of Service** and acknowledge the **Privacy Policy**. You cannot use CoopData until you do. Each acceptance is stored with the document version and the time.

## Finding your way around

The left menu has three groups. The pages you see depend on your role.

| Group | What is in it |
| --- | --- |
| Oversight | Dashboard, Submissions, and your organisation pages (Federations, Apexes, Cooperatives, Members, Invitations) |
| Intelligence | Reports, Analytics, Basic Analytics, Benchmarking, Basic Benchmarking (Custom KPIs for the Ministry) |
| System | Users and roles, Settings, Questionnaire forms, Audit log, Data privacy and consents, Profile |

A pill at the top shows whether you are online or offline. The theme (light or dark) and the language can be changed from the menu and from your profile.

## Your profile

Open **Profile** from the System group.

- **Language:** English, French, Portuguese and siSwati. The change is saved immediately.
- **Password:** enter the current password, then the new one. The checklist shows which rules are still missing.
- **Two-step verification (authenticator app):**
  1. Press **Enable**. You are taken to the secure sign-in page.
  2. Sign in again, scan the QR code with an authenticator app, and enter the code it shows.
  3. From then on, every sign-in asks for a code.
  - You can switch it off and on again later without scanning a new code.
  - If you change phone, use the change-device option. It asks for your password and a current code, then shows a new QR code.
- **Role scope:** a read-only list of what your role is allowed to do.

## Data privacy and consents

Open **Data privacy & consents** from the System group.

- The page lists every legal document you are bound by or may consent to, with its current version. Each tile opens the document in the legal centre.
- **Required to use CoopData:** Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.
- **Optional:** the remaining documents and the cookie preferences.
- The consent history shows every acceptance recorded for your account.

## Working offline

CoopData keeps working when the connection drops, so a cooperative in a weak-signal area can carry on.

| Works offline | Needs a connection |
| --- | --- |
| Open pages you have already visited | Signing in |
| See saved data | Submitting a return |
| Create a draft and fill it in | Approving or returning a submission |
| Edit a draft you hold | Delegating, reclaiming or claiming a submission |

- Changes made offline wait on your device and are sent automatically when the connection returns. The pill shows how many are waiting.
- If the server refuses a change, a red **Not saved** badge appears. Open it to retry or dismiss each item.
- Offline use needs a first online visit after signing in. The app downloads your data in the background right after sign-in. Stay online a short while the first time.
- A saved sign-in lasts 30 days from your last password sign-in. After that you need a connection to sign in again.
- Logging out while changes are still waiting shows a warning. Wait for them to send first.

## Signing out

Use **Logout** in the menu. After 10 minutes without activity (mouse, keyboard, touch or scroll) you are signed out for safety, without a prior warning. Saved drafts are kept.
