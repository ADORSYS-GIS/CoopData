# FAQ and troubleshooting

## Sign-in and account

**I did not receive the invitation email.**
Ask the person who invited you to resend it from their Users or Invitations page. Check the spam folder. The status "Awaiting verification" means the email address has not been verified yet.

**I lost my phone and cannot give a code.**
Contact the support address your organisation gave you. Your account needs its authenticator entry reset before you can sign in. When you have a working phone, use the change-device option on your profile to link it.

**I am told to accept the Terms of Service again.**
A required document was updated. Accept it to continue.

**The session ended while I was working.**
Sessions end after a period of inactivity. Sign in again. Drafts that were saved are still there.

## Submissions

**I cannot change the frequency or the period of a submission.**
They are fixed when the submission is created. Delete the draft and create a new one, or ask your apex to help.

**The system says the period already exists.**
Open the existing submission from the list instead of creating it again.

**I cannot choose a different frequency for this year.**
A year has one frequency. Delete the draft submissions of that year to change it. Approved or submitted ones cannot be mixed with another frequency.

**The submit button is disabled.**
Every mandatory section must be marked Ready. The page lists what remains.

**The AI extraction is slow or wrong.**
Extraction takes about a minute and a half. Keep the page open. Correct any wrong figure by hand before you mark the section Ready.

**I am not allowed to edit this submission.**
Only one person edits a draft at a time, and a submission already sent up is read-only. If your apex is preparing it, a banner says so.

## Analytics and reports

**A cooperative or a year is missing from a chart.**
Either the submissions are not all approved, or the year is not complete yet, or the cooperative is of the other type. See [How the numbers are built](analytics-explained.md).

**The dashboard shows more submissions than the analytics tab.**
The dashboard counts both cooperative types. Each analytics tab counts one type. The banner at the top says which.

**Growth looks different from what I expect.**
Growth compares complete years, and only cooperatives with a complete year in both years. A note says how many were left out.

**I cannot generate the report for this year.**
A year reported in several periods needs every period approved first.

## Offline

**The app says it is offline.**
You can keep working on drafts. Changes are sent when the connection returns. See [Getting started](getting-started.md#working-offline).

**A red "Not saved" badge appeared.**
The server refused a change made offline, for example because the submission was reclaimed. Open the badge to retry or dismiss it.

**My data is not available offline.**
Open the page once while online so it is saved on the device.

## Still stuck?

Contact the support address your organisation gave you. Give the page, the time, and the reference of the submission if there is one.
