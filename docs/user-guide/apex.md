# Apex guide

For apex officers. You manage a group of cooperatives and review their returns. You are the final approval level: when you approve a return it is approved for good, and nothing waits at the federation or the Ministry.

## What you can see

- **Dashboard:** counts of returns waiting for you, in review and approved.
- **Cooperatives:** the directory of cooperatives under your apex.
- **Submissions:** the review queue, grouped by cooperative.
- **Reports, Analytics, Basic Analytics, Benchmarking, Basic Benchmarking:** figures for your cooperatives.
- **Users:** the members of your apex.
- **Data privacy & consents** and **Profile**.

## Register and manage cooperatives

Open **Cooperatives**.

- The cards at the top show the total, active, inactive or suspended cooperatives, and the number of SACCOs.
- Search by name, registration number, region or sector.
- **Register cooperative** creates a new one. Set the **cooperative type** carefully: SACCO (statement upload or manual entry) or multipurpose (questionnaire). The type decides how the cooperative reports, and the sector offered must match the type. Do not change the type of a cooperative that already has submissions, because its past returns were made with the other method. A cooperative with no type is left out of the type-specific analytics.
- Open a cooperative to see its profile, its members and its submissions. You can edit its profile. A suspended cooperative cannot submit data.
- Deleting a cooperative removes what sits under it. The system asks for extra confirmation first. See [Deleting things](#deleting-things).

## Review a submission

1. Open **Submissions** and choose a cooperative.
2. Open a submission that was submitted and is waiting for you.
3. Read the data, the uploaded document if there is one, and the review history.
4. Decide:
   - **Approve** is final. The submission is locked and its figures feed the dashboards, analytics and reports. The federation and the Ministry only see it.
   - **Request Changes** sends it back to the cooperative as a draft. A comment is required, and the cooperative sees it. When the cooperative submits again, it comes back to your queue.

Filters on the queue show All, Draft, In review, Approved and Rejected. You review each period on its own with that period's own figures. The complete-year rule only affects analytics, not your review.

## Submit on a cooperative's behalf

You can create, fill and submit a return in place of a cooperative that cannot do it itself.

1. In **Submissions**, choose the cooperative and press **New Submission**.
2. Choose the method and fill in the data. You are the only editor. The cooperative sees the submission as read-only.
3. Press submit. Because you are the final approval level, **your own submission is approved immediately**. There is no further review.

### Hand a draft to the cooperative

For a draft you created, you can choose instead to let the cooperative fill it in:

| Option | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| **Delegate to Cooperative** | Editing passes to the cooperative. It completes the data and submits back to you. You then approve it or request changes |
| **Reclaim** | At any time while the draft is delegated, you take editing back. The cooperative loses edit access, and changes it had not saved are not kept |

A return that the cooperative created itself has no delegate or reclaim: you only approve it or request changes.

## Manage apex members

Open **Users**. You can invite a member with the **Apex Officer** role, resend a verification email, update a member and remove a member. A new member receives an email to verify the address and set a password.

## Deleting things

Deleting a cooperative, a user or a submission cannot be undone. The system asks you to:

1. For a cooperative or a user: type the exact name, after seeing how many members and records go with it.
2. Enter your password, and your authenticator code if enabled.

The confirmation is valid for 2 minutes and works once.

## Working offline

You can create and fill drafts offline. Approving, returning, delegating and reclaiming need a connection. See [Getting started](getting-started.md#working-offline).

## Download a report in your language

Reports are PDFs, available in English, French, Portuguese and siSwati. This applies to both report editions (financial statements and questionnaire).

1. Open **Reports** and choose what you want to export.
2. Pick the language. It starts on the language of the app.
3. Press **Download**. If the report is ready, it downloads at once.

If the language has not been prepared yet, the button shows "Preparing your report..." and the file downloads by itself when it is ready. This can take a short while, because the text is translated on request. English is always prepared first. If you close the page while it works, a notification with a Download button appears when the report is ready.

The small **Update report** link rebuilds the English report from the latest data. The other languages are then cleared and prepared again the next time someone asks for them. If preparation fails, a message asks you to try again; the button stays available.
