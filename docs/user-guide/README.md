# CoopData User Guide

This guide is for the people who use CoopData every day. It explains what each role sees, what each role can do, and how a return is reviewed and approved.

For installation, deployment and code, see the [developer documentation](../README.md).

## The organisation chain

```
Cooperative  ->  Apex                         Federation, Ministry
(files data)    (reviews, final approval)     (oversee, analyse, report)
```

- **The apex is the final approval level.** When an apex approves a submission it is approved for good. Nothing waits at the federation or the Ministry.
- A cooperative sends its return to its apex. The apex approves it, or sends it back to the cooperative with a comment.
- Federations and the Ministry do not approve. They see every submission below them, follow its status, and use the approved data in analytics, benchmarking and reports.
- Each level only sees its own part of the chain. An apex sees its cooperatives, a federation sees its apexes and their cooperatives, and the Ministry sees everything.
- On some screens the apex is called the regional office.

## The two kinds of cooperative

The type of a cooperative decides how it reports. It is set when the cooperative is registered.

| Type | Reports by | Analytics screens |
| --- | --- | --- |
| SACCO | Uploading a financial statement, or manual entry | Analytics, Benchmarking |
| Multipurpose | The questionnaire | Basic Analytics, Basic Benchmarking |

A short banner at the top of each analytics tab tells you which type of cooperative that tab covers. Counts of submissions and approvals are never merged across the two types on these screens.

## Terms used in this guide

| Term | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Submission | One return for one reporting period of one cooperative |
| Period | The slice of the year a submission covers: the year, a half-year, a quarter or a month |
| Frequency | How often a cooperative reports: yearly, half-yearly, quarterly or monthly |
| Complete year | A year in which every period of the cooperative's frequency is approved |
| Tier | The level currently holding a submission: the cooperative or its apex |
