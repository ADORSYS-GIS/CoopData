# Cooperative guide

For cooperative managers and the staff they invite. You own your cooperative's data: you prepare each return, send it to your apex, and fix it if the apex sends it back.

## What you can see

- **Dashboard:** your submissions at a glance, with a reminder when a reporting year is still missing periods.
- **Submissions:** every return of your cooperative, a **Reporting years** panel, and the New Submission button.
- **Reports, Analytics, Benchmarking:** your own figures. The screens that apply depend on your cooperative type (see [How the numbers are built](analytics-explained.md)).
- **Data privacy & consents** and **Profile**.

## Choose how you report

Your cooperative type decides the reporting method.

| Cooperative type | Method | What you provide |
| --- | --- | --- |
| SACCO | Upload documents, or Manual entry | Financial statement plus non-financial records |
| Multipurpose | Questionnaire | Answers to a guided set of questions |

When you open a new draft, a window asks you to **choose how to submit your data**. The choice is used for both the financial and the non-financial sections of that submission. You can change it while the submission is still a draft.

## Create a submission

1. Open **Submissions** and press **New Submission**.
2. Choose the **reporting year**, the **frequency** and the **period**.
3. Press **Create Submission**.

The frequency, the period and the start month are fixed once the submission exists. They cannot be edited later, so check them before you create it. The rules that protect you from mistakes:

- **One frequency per year.** If 2026 is already reported quarterly, you can only add quarters to 2026. To change the frequency, delete your draft submissions of that year first.
- **No duplicates.** A period that already exists cannot be created again. Open the existing one from the list.
- **Quarters keep the same start month** within a year, so periods never overlap.
- **A full-year return and single months cannot be mixed** in the same year.

### A year counts only when it is complete

Dashboards, analytics, benchmarking and reports use a year only when every period of your frequency is approved: all four quarters, both halves, all twelve months, or the yearly return. The **Reporting years** panel shows one pill per period (approved, waiting for the apex, draft or not started) and tells you whether the year is "Complete · counted" or, for example, "2 of 4 approved · Not counted yet".

A bell icon lists reporting reminders for any year that still has periods to submit.

## Fill in a submission

Open the submission from the list. The **Submission Readiness Center** lists the sections you must complete. Each one is marked **Ready** when done, and **Submit to Apex** unlocks when all mandatory sections are ready.

### Upload documents (SACCO)

1. Upload the financial statement (PDF or Excel) and the non-financial Excel files.
2. The system reads the document and maps the values to the standard chart of accounts. This takes about a minute and a half. Keep the page open.
3. Check every extracted figure. Correct anything wrong. The system flags values that look inconsistent, for example a balance sheet that does not balance.
4. Mark the section Ready.

### Manual entry (SACCO)

Enter the financial statement row by row and field by field. The period bar at the top shows the frequency, period and months covered, and is read-only. Then enter the non-financial records: members, savings, loans, fixed deposits and farm data where it applies.

### Questionnaire (multipurpose)

Answer the guided Financial and Non-Financial questionnaires. The system turns your answers into the submission and computes the indicators for you. Amounts are entered in SZL.

### Saving

Your work is saved as you go. You can leave and come back to a draft. Offline, drafts are kept on your device and sent when the connection returns (see [Getting started](getting-started.md#working-offline)).

## Send it for review

Press **Submit to Apex**. The submission goes to your apex for review. From then on you can read it but not edit it. The apex is the final approval level: your federation and the Ministry see the return but do not approve it.

You can delete a submission you created yourself as long as it is not approved yet. Deleting asks for your password (and your authenticator code if enabled). An approved submission cannot be deleted from this screen.

## When it comes back

If your apex asks for changes, the submission goes back to you as a draft and the apex's comment appears in the **Review History**. Read the comment, correct the data and submit again. The history keeps every comment and decision.

## When your apex prepares a return for you

An apex may create and fill a return on your behalf. You then see a banner saying the apex is preparing it, and the submission is read-only for you. If the apex hands the draft to you to complete (delegates it), you can edit it, and you submit it back to the apex when done. The apex then approves it or sends it back. If the apex takes the draft back, a banner tells you editing is closed. Contact the apex officer named in the banner if something still needs to change.

Only one person edits a draft at a time. If a colleague has it open, you see who.

## Download a report in your language

Reports are PDFs, available in English, French, Portuguese and siSwati. This applies to both report editions (financial statements and questionnaire).

1. Open **Reports** and choose what you want to export.
2. Pick the language. It starts on the language of the app.
3. Press **Download**. If the report is ready, it downloads at once.

If the language has not been prepared yet, the button shows "Preparing your report..." and the file downloads by itself when it is ready. This can take a short while, because the text is translated on request. English is always prepared first. If you close the page while it works, a notification with a Download button appears when the report is ready.

The small **Update report** link rebuilds the English report from the latest data. The other languages are then cleared and prepared again the next time someone asks for them. If preparation fails, a message asks you to try again; the button stays available.

## Where your data goes

When your apex approves the return, it is locked and final. The approval is complete: no one above the apex has to approve it. Its figures then feed the dashboards, analytics, benchmarking and reports. Figures of an approved period never change. See [How the numbers are built](analytics-explained.md).
